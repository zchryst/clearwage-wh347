1. Nine Structural Changes Between the Old WH-347 and the Mandatory 2026 Layout

Form Section Expired Pre-2025 WH-347 Mandatory Oct 2026 WH-347 (Rev. Jan 2025)
Top Header No Final Payroll checkbox; ambiguous role Dedicated Submission of Final DBRA Certified Payroll, Prime, and Subcontractor checkboxes + mandatory SAM.gov Wage Determination No. box.
Columns 1A–1E Single "Name and Individual Identifying Number" box Split into 5 columns: 1A Entry No., 1B Last, 1C First, 1D MI, and 1E 4-digit Worker ID (full SSNs strictly prohibited).
Split-Trade Workers Unstandardized row numbering Multiple classifications for one worker must share the same Col 1A Entry No., reporting Cols 7B, 8 & 9 once without double-counting.

Columns 7A & 7B Stacked slash cell Dedicated 7A Gross Amt Earned (Project) and 7B Gross Amt Earned for All Work columns.
Page 2 Box 4 (RA) Narrative exceptions text Structured 3-row OA / SAA Registered Apprenticeship Table; requires writing "N/A" when Box 4 is unchecked.
