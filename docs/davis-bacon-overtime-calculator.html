Davis-Bacon & CWHSSA Overtime Formula: How the Half-Time Premium & Fringe Rules Work

Standard commercial payroll software frequently miscalculates Davis-Bacon overtime in one of two costly ways: either multiplying cash-in-lieu fringe benefits by 1.5× (overpaying labor costs by thousands of dollars) or multiplying an artificially low cash base rate by 1.5× (triggering back-wage withholding and $32/day CWHSSA liquidated damages under 29 CFR § 5.5(b)(2)).

The Exact 29 CFR § 5.32 Overtime Equation

Minimum Total Overtime Package ($/hr) =
[ 1.0 × Regular/WD Basic Hourly Rate ] + [ 0.5 × max(SAM.gov WD Basic Rate, Actual Regular Base Rate) ] + [ 1.0 × Full Prevailing Hourly Fringe ]
  • Fringes are owed on ALL hours (ST + OT) at 1.0×, NOT 1.5×: Under CWHSSA, prevailing fringe benefits must be paid for every overtime hour worked, but the 0.5× overtime premium applies strictly to the basic/regular rate — never to bona fide plan credits or properly segregated Col 6C cash-in-lieu fringes.
  • Paying above the SAM.gov basic rate raises the FLSA regular rate: If the Wage Determination sets Electrician basic pay at $38.50/hr + $14.20/hr fringe, and you pay an Electrician $39.00/hr base cash, their minimum Col 6A OT cash rate is 1.5 × $39.00 = $58.50/hr (plus $14.20/hr fringe = $72.70/hr total package).
  • Split-Classification >40 Hour Trap: If one employee works 24 hours as an Electrician and 22 hours as an HVAC Mechanic on the same federal contract (46 hours total), CWHSSA mandates at least 6 hours of overtime — even though neither trade exceeded 40 hours alone!
