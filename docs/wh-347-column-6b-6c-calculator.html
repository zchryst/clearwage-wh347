How to Calculate Form WH-347 Columns 6A, 6B, 6C & 7A Without Rejection

The single most common mathematical rejection on the Revised January 2025 Form WH-347 is mixing hourly rates with weekly dollar totals across Columns 6A, 6B, and 6C — or including cash paid in lieu of fringe benefits inside Column 6A.

Exact Statutory Formulas for Each Column

  • Column 6A — Hourly Wage Rate Paid for ST and OT ($/hr): Enter ONLY the actual hourly cash base wage paid for straight time (top) and overtime (bottom). Never include cash paid in lieu of fringe benefits in Column 6A.
  • Column 6B — Total Fringe Benefit Credit (Weekly $ Total): Equal to Total Weekly Hours (ST + OT) × Page 2 Total Hourly Plan Credit contributed to bona fide funded plans (health, pension, apprenticeship) or DOL-approved unfunded plans (29 CFR § 5.28).
  • Column 6C — Payment in Lieu of Fringe Benefits (Weekly $ Total): Equal to Total Weekly Hours (ST + OT) × Hourly Cash-in-Lieu Rate paid directly to the worker in cash wages.
  • Column 7A — Gross Amount Earned on Project (Weekly $ Total): Equal to (ST Hours × Col 6A ST Rate) + (OT Hours × Col 6A OT Rate) + Col 6C Cash-in-Lieu. Note that Col 6B plan contributions go to third-party trusts, so Col 6B is not added to Col 7A taxable cash gross!
Worked Subcontractor Example (General Laborer):
SAM.gov Wage Determination requires $22.40/hr basic wage + $7.60/hr fringe ($30.00/hr total package). Worker logs 40 ST hours. Employer contributes $4.00/hr to medical insurance and pays the remaining $3.60/hr in cash:
• Col 6A (ST): 22.40
• Col 6B (Weekly Plan Credit): 40 hrs × $4.00 = $160.00 (backed by $4.00 on Page 2)
• Col 6C (Weekly Cash-in-Lieu): 40 hrs × $3.60 = $144.00
• Col 7A (Project Gross): (40 × $22.40) + $144.00 = $1,040.00

The Page 2 "100% Cash-in-Lieu" Rule

Under official DOL Page 2 Box 5 instructions, if a contractor satisfies 100% of fringe obligations in cash (Col 6B = $0.00 and Col 6C > $0.00), check Box 5 on Page 2 and leave the Hourly Credit for Fringe Benefits table blank. Populate the Page 2 table only when claiming third-party plan credit in Column 6B.
